The Lore

How a good boy became the Dark Knight's right paw.

01

The Stray of Gotham

Every night the Bat swung over the rooftops. Every night a small shiba followed below, dodging rain, villains and rent. Nobody noticed. The doge noticed everything.

02

The Night He Was Seen

One night the Bat fell. Grapple snapped, cape tangled. The doge didn't bark. He jumped. Caught the cape in his teeth and pulled. Batman looked down and said one word: “Partner.”

03

The Suit

No cape, because capes get caught. Black for the shadows. A blue wing across the chest so the city would know: this one flies on his own.

04

Leaving the Nest

Every great partner eventually goes solo. Gotham was too small, so Nightwing Doge leapt to a chain fast enough to keep up with him: NEAR. Cheap gas, quick finality, no bats required.
